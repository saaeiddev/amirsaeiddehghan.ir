01 — The Artist

Biography

Amir Saeid Dehghan is a Multimedia Artist born in Tehran in 1998. He was educated in Directing, developing his creative practice across visual storytelling, digital art, graphic design, cinematography, animation, VFX and interactive experiences.

His work explores the intersection of art and technology, transforming ideas into cinematic visual experiences and imaginative digital worlds. With a background rooted in filmmaking and visual storytelling, Amir approaches every project as an opportunity to combine artistic direction, design and emerging technologies.
